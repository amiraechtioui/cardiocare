About CardioCare

CardioCare is a platform designed to assist in the early detection of cardiovascular risks. Using cutting-edge technology, our goal is to provide individuals with a simple yet effective way to assess their heart health and take preventive measures early.

Our tool analyzes user-provided data to predict potential cardiovascular issues and provides recommendations for lifestyle adjustments or medical consultation if necessary.
